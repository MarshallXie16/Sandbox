## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
**Risk**: Calculation errors damage credibility
**Mitigation**:
- Extensive test suite
- Manual verification against known valuations
- Clear disclaimers
- Regular audits

**Risk**: Poor performance on mobile
**Mitigation**:
- Mobile-first design
- Performance testing
- Progressive enhancement
- Lazy loading

### Market Risks
**Risk**: Low user adoption
**Mitigation**:
- Strong launch marketing
- SEO optimization
- Community engagement
- Referral incentives

**Risk**: Competition from established players
**Mitigation**:
- Focus on simplicity and UX
- Free tier always available
- Rapid iteration
- Niche targeting

### Business Risks
**Risk**: Legal liability for valuations
**Mitigation**:
- Clear disclaimers everywhere
- Terms of service
- Professional liability insurance (future)
- Position as educational tool
